there is always you.

For Isla,

I wish there were a script for me to follow when trying to express myself.

Even if there were, it would never come close to describing just how perfect you are.

I don't know what I'm doing.

Really.

Sometimes words simply aren't enough to convey just how much you can feel for someone.

That's me.

If it were up to me, I wouldn't hesitate to hand you this letter in person, if I ever had the chance.

But that would somehow limit what I wanted this to be, no matter how traditional or novel-like it might have been.

So I tried this instead. <3

I didn't want to limit myself to simply writing down how I feel about you.

I wanted to leave you something you could slowly make your way through.

Something where our memories could remain, the songs that remind me of you, the things I never found the right moment to say, and all those little thoughts that appear whenever there is an us.

I don't know if this will be enough to explore everything I want to tell you.

Probably not.

But what I do hope is that it can show you just how much you've come to mean to me since you appeared in my life.

Because I haven't been the same since then.

So I'm giving this to you.

A little piece of me, made for you.

A little piece that constantly beats for you. <3

A little piece that bears your name.

Isla.

— Daniel
